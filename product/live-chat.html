Live Chat

Chat that loads fast and never leaves a customer waiting.

Relaydesk Live Chat is an embeddable website widget of about 38 KB gzipped. It loads asynchronously, speaks 32 languages, and quietly turns into email when your team is offline.

Start free trialTalk to sales

Features

Everything a website chat should do.

Live Chat is included on every plan, including Starter. Every conversation lands in the Shared Inbox next to email and messaging, with the same macros, tags and reports.

Lightweight widget

About 38 KB gzipped and loaded asynchronously, so your pages render first and the chat button appears right after.

32 widget languages

The widget interface is available in 32 languages, so visitors see buttons, prompts and forms in their own language.

Proactive messages

Start the conversation on the pages that matter, like pricing or checkout, before a visitor gives up and leaves.

Pre-chat forms

Ask for a name, email or order number before the chat starts, so agents have context from the first message.

Offline mode

When nobody is available, chats become email tickets in the Shared Inbox. No missed messages, no “sorry, we're closed”.

Typing preview

Agents see what a customer is typing before they hit send, so the answer is often ready when the question arrives.

How it works

Live on your site in an afternoon.

  1. Style the widget

    Choose colors, greeting and language, and decide which pre-chat form fields to ask for.

  2. Paste the snippet

    Add one script tag to your site. Step-by-step guide: install the chat widget.

  3. Set your hours

    Attach a business-hours schedule so chat switches to offline mode outside working hours.

  4. Answer and follow up

    Reply from the Shared Inbox or the iOS and Android agent apps, and email the transcript to the customer automatically.

Details and limits

The numbers behind the widget.

  • Widget size about 38 KB gzipped, loaded asynchronously.
  • 32 widget languages.
  • File attachments up to 25 MB per file.
  • Mobile SDKs for iOS and Android to add chat inside your own app.
  • Chat transcripts can be emailed to the customer automatically.
  • Offline chats become email tickets, measured against your SLA policies like any other ticket.

Chat that works with the rest of Relaydesk

Every chat becomes a ticket in the Shared Inbox, so it shares tags, views, macros, assignment rules and SLA policies with email and messaging. When a chat ends, Relaydesk can email the transcript to the customer automatically, and the CSAT survey goes out 1 hour after the ticket is solved.

Add Relay AI when you are ready

On Growth and Scale, Relay AI Copilot drafts chat replies for agents, and Autopilot can answer website visitors directly from your Knowledge Base articles, handing off to a person when its confidence is low or the visitor asks. FAQ

Live Chat questions

Will the chat widget slow down my website?

The widget is about 38 KB gzipped and loads asynchronously, so it does not block your page from rendering.

Which plans include Live Chat?

All of them. Live Chat is included on Starter, Growth and Scale. Relay AI in chat requires Growth or Scale.

What happens if nobody is online?

Offline mode turns the chat into an email ticket in the Shared Inbox, so the customer gets an answer by email instead of an empty chat window.

Can I add chat to my mobile app?

Yes. Live Chat has mobile SDKs for iOS and Android, and those conversations land in the same Shared Inbox.
