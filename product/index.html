The Relaydesk platform

Four products, one calm inbox.

Relaydesk is an omnichannel helpdesk for small and mid-size businesses. Shared Inbox, Live Chat, Knowledge Base and Relay AI share one customer record, one set of reports and one place for your team to work.

The four products

Pick what you need today. Everything works together.

Each product is useful on its own, but they are designed to share context. A chat that goes offline becomes an email ticket, a Knowledge Base article becomes an answer Relay AI can send, and every conversation lands in the same queue.

Shared Inbox

Email, SMS, WhatsApp and Facebook and Instagram DMs in one queue. Collision detection, internal notes with @mentions, macros, tags, views and assignment rules keep a growing team from stepping on each other.

Explore Shared Inbox

Live Chat

An embeddable website widget of about 38 KB gzipped that loads asynchronously, speaks 32 languages, and turns chats into email tickets when nobody is online. Mobile SDKs for iOS and Android.

Explore Live Chat

Knowledge Base

A public help center on your own domain with article versioning, internal-only articles, translations in 18 languages, search analytics and thumbs-up or thumbs-down article feedback.

Explore Knowledge Base

Relay AI

Copilot drafts replies and summarizes threads for your agents. Autopilot answers customers directly from your Knowledge Base and hands off to a human when confidence is low or the customer asks.

Explore Relay AI

Reporting and SLAs

Reporting and SLAs across everything.

Reporting is not a separate product. Dashboards, SLA policies and CSAT surveys are built into every part of Relaydesk, so a WhatsApp thread, a website chat and an email ticket are measured against the same targets.

SLA policies set targets for first reply time, next reply time and full resolution time. Attach a business-hours schedule and the clock only runs while your team is working, with holiday calendars per schedule. Starter includes 1 SLA policy and 1 business-hours schedule; Growth includes unlimited SLA policies and up to 5 schedules; Scale includes unlimited of both. Since the 2026.10 release, SLA breach forecasts warn you before a target is missed.

  • Dashboards that cover every channel and every product in one place
  • SLA targets for first reply, next reply and full resolution, in business hours when a schedule is attached
  • SLA breach forecasts that flag tickets at risk before they breach
  • CSAT survey sent 1 hour after a ticket is solved, configurable from 0 to 72 hours
  • CSAT on a 5-point scale
  • Full workspace export in CSV or JSON, available for 7 days after generation
