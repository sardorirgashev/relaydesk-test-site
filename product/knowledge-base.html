Knowledge Base

Answer the question before it becomes a ticket.

Relaydesk Knowledge Base is a public help center on your own domain, with versioned articles, internal-only notes for your team, and translations in 18 languages.

Features

A help center your team will keep up to date.

Every article is also a source for Relay AI, so a better help center means better self-service and better automated answers at the same time.

Your own domain

Publish the help center on a custom domain such as help.yourcompany.com, with your logo and colors.

Article versioning

Every published change is saved as a version. Compare edits and restore an earlier version if something goes wrong.

Internal-only articles

Keep troubleshooting steps, policies and escalation paths for your team only, right next to the public articles.

18 languages

Translate articles into 18 languages and serve each reader the version that matches their language.

Search analytics

See what readers search for, and the “searches with no results” report tells you exactly which article to write next.

Article feedback

Readers rate each article with a thumbs up or thumbs down, so you know which pages actually help.

How it works

Write once, help everywhere.

  1. Write

    Draft articles in the editor and mark each one public or internal-only.

  2. Translate

    Add translations in any of the 18 supported languages.

  3. Publish

    Go live on your custom domain. Each change is saved as a new version.

  4. Improve

    Use searches with no results and thumbs-down feedback to decide what to fix or write next.

Your articles power Relay AI

Relay AI Autopilot answers customers on chat and email using your Knowledge Base articles, and hands off to a person when its confidence is low or the customer asks. FAQ

Knowledge Base questions

How many Knowledge Bases can I create?

Starter includes 1 Knowledge Base, Growth includes 3 and Scale includes unlimited. Multiple Knowledge Bases are useful for separate brands or products.

How many languages are supported for translations?

18 languages. The 2026.08 release on August 19, 2026 raised the number from 12 to 18.

Can I hide some articles from the public?

Yes. Mark an article internal-only and it is visible to your team in Relaydesk but never appears on the public help center.

Can I undo a bad edit?

Yes. Article versioning saves every published change, so you can compare versions and restore an earlier one.

Does Relay AI use my Knowledge Base?

Yes. Relay AI Autopilot answers customers using your Knowledge Base articles. Autopilot resolutions are included on Growth (500 per month) and Scale (2,000 per month).
