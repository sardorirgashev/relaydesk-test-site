Features

Built for teams that share the work.

The Shared Inbox is the center of Relaydesk. Every other product feeds into it, and every feature is designed to stop two agents answering the same customer twice.

Every channel, one queue

Email, SMS, WhatsApp and Facebook and Instagram DMs show up as tickets in the same list, with the full customer history on the right.

Collision detection

See “Ana is replying…” the moment a teammate opens a reply, so nobody sends a second answer to the same question.

Internal notes and @mentions

Leave a note the customer never sees and @mention a colleague to pull them in. Notes stay attached to the ticket.

Macros

Saved replies for the questions you answer every day. Insert a macro, adjust a line, send. How macros work.

Tags and views

Tag tickets by topic and build views such as “VIP, unassigned” or “Refund requests this week” that every agent can pin.

Assignment rules

Route work round-robin, load-balanced or skills-based, so the right agent gets the ticket without a manager triaging by hand.

How it works

From first message to solved.

  1. Connect your channels

    Forward your support address to support-<workspace>@inbound.relaydesk.example, then add SMS, WhatsApp and social accounts on Growth or Scale.

  2. Route automatically

    Assignment rules hand each new ticket to the right person or team the moment it arrives.

  3. Reply together

    Use macros, internal notes and collision detection to answer quickly without duplicate replies.

  4. Solve and measure

    Solved tickets auto-close after 4 days without a customer reply, and a CSAT survey goes out 1 hour after solving.

FAQ

Shared Inbox questions

Can I keep my existing support email address?

Yes. Forward it to your workspace address, support-<workspace>@inbound.relaydesk.example. To send replies from your own domain, add SPF and DKIM records; we also recommend DMARC.

Does the Starter plan include WhatsApp or SMS?

No. Starter covers email (2 inboxes) and Live Chat. SMS, WhatsApp and Facebook and Instagram DMs are included on Growth and Scale.

What happens when a customer replies to an old ticket?

If the ticket is solved, the reply reopens it. Once a ticket is closed, which happens 4 days after solving by default, a reply creates a new ticket linked to the original.

How do assignment rules decide who gets a ticket?

Choose round-robin to rotate evenly, load-balanced to favor the agent with the fewest open tickets, or skills-based to match tickets to agents by skill. See assignment rules.
