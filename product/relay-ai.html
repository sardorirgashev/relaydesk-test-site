Relay AI

AI that answers the easy questions and knows when to hand off.

Relay AI, the AI assistant built into Relaydesk, works in two modes: Copilot helps your agents write faster, and Autopilot answers customers directly from your Knowledge Base.

Start free trialTalk to sales

Two modes

Copilot for your team. Autopilot for your customers.

Copilot

Copilot sits inside the ticket and works for the agent. It drafts a reply the agent can edit and send, and summarizes long threads so anyone picking up a ticket understands it in seconds. Nothing reaches the customer until an agent sends it. Copilot is available on Growth and Scale and first launched in 2024.05.

Autopilot

Autopilot answers customers directly on chat and email using your Knowledge Base articles, and on WhatsApp since the 2026.10 release. It hands off to a human when its confidence is low or the customer asks for a person. Autopilot has been generally available since 2025.11. Read the announcement.

Features

Grounded, careful and easy to supervise.

Draft replies

Copilot writes a first draft of the reply. Agents edit, approve and send.

Thread summaries

Turn a long back-and-forth into a short summary when a ticket changes hands.

Autopilot answers

Resolve common questions on chat, email and WhatsApp without an agent touching them.

Grounded in your articles

Autopilot answers from your Knowledge Base, so improving an article improves the answers.

Human handoff

Low confidence or a customer request for a person sends the conversation to your team with the context attached.

Private by design

Relay AI is never trained on customer data across workspaces. Your conversations do not improve anyone else's answers.

How it works

From first draft to full automation.

  1. Prepare your articles

    Autopilot answers from your Knowledge Base, so start with your most-asked questions.

  2. Turn on Copilot

    Let agents use drafts and summaries while you review the quality of answers.

  3. Enable Autopilot

    Switch it on for chat, email or WhatsApp. Setup guide: set up Relay AI.

  4. Review and improve

    Check handoffs and resolutions, then fix the articles behind weak answers.

FAQ

Relay AI questions

What counts as an AI resolution?

A conversation Autopilot closes with no human reply in the following 72 hours. Conversations handed off to an agent, or answered by an agent within 72 hours, do not count.

What happens if we use more than our included resolutions?

Each AI resolution beyond your plan's monthly allowance (500 on Growth, 2,000 on Scale) costs $0.60. Extra AI resolutions are never refunded.

When does Autopilot hand off to a human?

When its confidence in an answer is low, or when the customer asks to talk to a person.

Is our data used to train AI for other companies?

No. Relay AI is never trained on customer data across workspaces.

Is Relay AI available on Starter?

No. Copilot and Autopilot resolutions are included on Growth and Scale. You can try both during the 14-day free trial, which includes 50 AI resolutions.
