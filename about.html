About us

Support should feel calm.

Relaydesk started in 2019 in a Portland coworking space, when Maya Okafor and Daniel Reyes watched a five-person bike shop lose a weekend to a single shipping delay. Three inboxes, two chat tools and a sticky-note queue. Nobody knew who had replied to whom.

We built the tool we wished they had: one queue, one customer timeline, and enough automation to give people back the time to be kind.

What we believe

Four principles we build by.

One queue beats five

Every channel lands in the same place with the same rules. Context should never depend on where a customer wrote in.

Humans for the hard part

AI should take the repetitive questions and step aside the moment a person is the better answer.

Your data is not our product

We don’t sell data and we don’t train shared AI models on your customers’ conversations.

Small teams first

Every feature has to work for a team of three before it ships for a team of three hundred.
