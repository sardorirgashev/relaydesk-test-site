Billing and invoices

Everything about paying for Relaydesk in one place: what each plan costs, how seat changes and downgrades are billed, how to pay, where to find invoices, and how refunds work under the current policy.

Last updated September 30, 2026 · 3 min read

Plans and prices

Relaydesk is priced per agent seat. Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax.

PlanMonthly billingAnnual billing (per month)Max agent seats
Starter$19$1510
Growth$49$39Unlimited
Scale$89$71Unlimited

Annual billing saves 20% (rounded) and is charged up front for the year. For example, 8 Growth seats cost 8 × $49 = $392 per month on monthly billing, or 8 × $39 × 12 = $3,744 per year on annual billing. Light (collaborator) seats are free on all plans. They can view tickets and add internal notes but cannot reply to customers. Starter allows up to 50 light seats.

Growth and Scale also include Relay AI Autopilot resolutions: 500 per month on Growth and 2,000 on Scale. Extra AI resolutions are billed at $0.60 each at the end of the month. The full feature comparison is on the pricing page.

Every new workspace starts with a 14-day free trial of Growth features, including 50 AI resolutions. No credit card is required. Add a payment method before the trial ends to keep your workspace active.

Changing seats and plans

  • Adding seats: seats added mid-cycle are prorated. You pay only for the remaining part of the current month or year, and the charge appears on your next invoice.
  • Removing seats: seats removed take effect at your next renewal. You can keep using them until then.
  • Upgrading: takes effect immediately, and the price difference for the rest of the period is prorated.
  • Downgrading: takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Until then you keep your current plan's features. Before a downgrade to Starter, reduce to 10 agent seats, 2 email inboxes, 1 Knowledge Base, and 1 SLA policy, because those are Starter's limits.

Only the workspace owner and admins with the Billing permission can change plans and seats, under Settings → Billing.

Payment methods

All plans can pay by card: Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Invoice, ACH, and wire payment are available on annual Scale plans only. To switch an annual Scale plan to invoicing, email sales@relaydesk.example or call +1 (503) 555-0142 (Monday–Friday, 8am–6pm Pacific). Invoices on annual Scale are due within 30 days.

Tax

Prices exclude tax. Relaydesk adds sales tax, VAT, or GST where required, based on your billing address. If your business is registered for VAT or GST, add your tax ID under Settings → Billing → Tax details before your next charge. It appears on every invoice, and reverse charge applies where eligible.

Nonprofit and education discount

Registered nonprofits and accredited education institutions get 40% off Growth and Scale on annual billing. To apply, contact sales@relaydesk.example from your organization's email domain with proof of status. The discount is applied from your next annual purchase or renewal. It does not apply to monthly billing, Starter, or extra AI resolutions.

Download invoices

  1. Go to Settings → Billing → Invoices.
  2. Find the invoice by date. Each row shows the amount, status (Paid, Due, or Refunded), and plan.
  3. Click Download PDF. Receipts for card payments are available from the same row.

Invoices are also emailed to your billing email address as soon as they are issued.

Change your billing email

  1. Go to Settings → Billing → Billing contact.
  2. Enter the new address, for example a shared finance mailbox. You can add up to 3 CC addresses.
  3. Click Save. Future invoices, receipts, and payment reminders go to the new address.

Refunds

Refunds follow the current refund policy, effective March 1, 2026:

  • Monthly plans: full refund of the first paid charge if you request it within 30 days. Later monthly charges are not refunded.
  • Annual plans: full refund if you request it within 30 days of purchase or renewal. After 30 days there is no refund, but you keep access until the end of your term.
  • Extra AI resolutions and SMS usage are never refunded.

To request a refund, go to Settings → Billing and click Request refund, or email billing@relaydesk.example. Approved refunds reach your card in 5–10 business days.

Tip: If you are leaving Relaydesk, export your data before your access ends.
