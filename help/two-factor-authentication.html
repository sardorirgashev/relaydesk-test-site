Set up two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) asks for a second proof of identity when you sign in, so a stolen password alone isn't enough to get into your account.

Last updated August 27, 2026 · 3 min read

Relaydesk supports two kinds of second factor. You can register both, and we recommend you do, so you always have a backup.

  • Authenticator app (TOTP). An app such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password shows a 6-digit code that changes every 30 seconds.
  • Security key (WebAuthn). A hardware key such as a YubiKey, or a built-in authenticator such as Touch ID or Windows Hello. You tap the key or use your fingerprint instead of typing a code, and it can't be phished.

Turn on 2FA with an authenticator app

  1. Click your avatar and choose Profile → Security.
  2. Under Two-factor authentication, click Add authenticator app.
  3. Scan the QR code with your app. If you can't scan it, enter the setup key shown below the code.
  4. Type the 6-digit code from the app to confirm.
  5. Save your recovery codes (see below), then click Done.

Add a security key

  1. Go to Profile → Security and click Add security key.
  2. Give the key a name you'll recognize, such as "Blue YubiKey" or "MacBook Touch ID".
  3. Follow your browser's prompt to insert and tap the key, or use your fingerprint or face.

You can register more than one key. Keep a spare in a safe place in case you lose your main one.

Recovery codes

When you turn on 2FA, Relaydesk gives you 10 recovery codes. Each code can be used once, in place of an authenticator code or security key, if you lose access to your device.

  • Download or print the codes and store them somewhere safe, such as your password manager.
  • Each time you use a code, it stops working. Your remaining count is shown on Profile → Security.
  • Click Generate new codes to get a fresh set of 10. Your old codes stop working immediately.
Important: Relaydesk support can't read your recovery codes to you. If you lose them and your device, only a workspace Admin can reset your 2FA.

Require 2FA for your workspace

Admins can require two-factor authentication for everyone in the workspace.

  1. Go to Settings → Security → Two-factor authentication.
  2. Turn on Require 2FA for all members.
  3. Optionally choose Security keys only if your policy doesn't allow authenticator apps.

Members who haven't set up 2FA are asked to do so the next time they sign in, and they can't open any tickets until they have. New members set up 2FA as part of accepting their invitation. The Team page shows a 2FA column so you can see who has it turned on.

If your workspace is on Scale and enforces SAML single sign-on, 2FA is handled by your identity provider instead, and the Relaydesk 2FA setting doesn't apply to SSO sign-ins.

Lost or replaced device

If you still have a backup: sign in with a recovery code or a registered security key. Then go to Profile → Security, remove the lost device and add your new one.

If you have no backup: ask a workspace Admin to go to Settings → Team, open your profile and click Reset 2FA. You'll be asked to set up 2FA again the next time you sign in. The reset is recorded in the audit log.

If you're the only Admin or the Owner and have no backup: email support@relaydesk.example from the address you use for Relaydesk. We'll verify your identity before resetting 2FA, which can take a few business days, so please keep your recovery codes safe.
