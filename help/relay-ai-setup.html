Set up Relay AI

Relay AI is the AI assistant built into Relaydesk. This guide walks admins through turning on Copilot and Autopilot, choosing what Relay AI is allowed to read, deciding when it must hand off to a person, and keeping an eye on usage.

Last updated October 15, 2026 · 4 min read

Copilot and Autopilot

Relay AI has two modes, and you can use either one or both.

  • Copilot works for your agents. It drafts replies in the composer and summarizes long threads in the ticket sidebar. An agent always reviews and sends the reply, so nothing reaches a customer without a person.
  • Autopilot works for your customers. It answers directly on Live Chat and email, and on WhatsApp since release 2026.10, using the Knowledge Base articles you choose. When it cannot help, it hands the conversation to a human.

Relay AI is available on Growth and Scale. Starter does not include Copilot or Autopilot. The 14-day free trial includes Growth features and 50 AI resolutions, so you can test Autopilot before you buy. For an overview, see the Relay AI product page.

Turn on Relay AI

  1. As an admin, go to Settings → Relay AI.
  2. Switch on Copilot. Drafts and summaries appear for every agent right away.
  3. To use Autopilot, choose your Knowledge Base sources (see below), then pick the channels and inboxes where Autopilot may answer.
  4. Review the handoff rules and tone settings.
  5. Click Test Autopilot to ask it sample questions in a private preview, then switch Autopilot on.
Tip: Start Autopilot on a single chat widget or inbox. Watch the first week of conversations in the Autopilot view before expanding to more channels.

Choose Knowledge Base sources

Autopilot only answers from the sources you select. Pick one or more Knowledge Bases, then narrow them to specific collections if needed. Your plan sets the number of Knowledge Bases: 1 on Starter, 3 on Growth, and unlimited on Scale.

  • Public articles can be used by both Copilot and Autopilot.
  • Internal-only articles can be used by Copilot to draft replies for agents, but are never quoted to customers by Autopilot.

Each Autopilot answer links to the articles it used, and agents can see those sources on the ticket. If customers ask something with no good article, the conversation is handed off, and the question appears in your Knowledge Base search analytics under "searches with no results". That list is the best place to find your next article to write.

Handoff rules

Autopilot hands a conversation to a human in three situations:

  1. Low confidence. If Autopilot is not confident its answer is supported by your sources, it does not guess. Set the threshold to Cautious, Balanced (default), or Assertive.
  2. The customer asks for a human. Phrases like "talk to a person" or "agent please" always trigger a handoff, in any language.
  3. Sensitive topics. Billing disputes, legal threats, account cancellation, and complaints about harassment or safety are handed off by default. Add your own topics or keywords to the list.

On handoff, Autopilot tells the customer a teammate will follow up, adds an internal note summarizing the conversation, and sends the ticket through your normal assignment rules. SLA timers start from the moment of handoff.

Tone settings

Choose a tone of Friendly, Neutral, or Formal, and add custom instructions of up to 500 characters, for example "Call customers by their first name. Never promise delivery dates." Autopilot replies in the customer's language. Copilot uses the same tone for its drafts, and agents can ask it to make a draft shorter, friendlier, or more formal.

What counts as an AI resolution

An AI resolution is a conversation that Autopilot closes with no human reply in the following 72 hours. Only AI resolutions count toward your usage.

  • A conversation Autopilot hands off is not an AI resolution.
  • If a customer comes back within 72 hours and an agent replies, the conversation is no longer counted.
  • Copilot drafts and summaries are never counted.

Usage alerts

Relaydesk emails workspace admins when the workspace reaches 80% and 100% of its included monthly resolutions. The current count is always visible under Settings → Relay AI → Usage. Under When included resolutions run out, choose to keep Autopilot running at $0.60 per extra resolution, or to pause Autopilot until next month. Paused Autopilot hands every new conversation to your team, and Copilot keeps working.

Note: Extra AI resolutions are never refunded. See Billing and invoices.

Your data

Relay AI is never trained on customer data across workspaces. Your tickets and articles are used only to answer within your own workspace. More detail is on our security page.
