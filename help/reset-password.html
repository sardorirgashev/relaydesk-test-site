Reset your password

Forgot your password? You can reset it yourself from the sign-in page in a couple of minutes.

Last updated June 3, 2026 · 2 min read

To reset your password, go to the sign-in page at app.relaydesk.example and click “Forgot password?”. Enter the email address you use for Relaydesk and we'll email you a reset link. The link expires after 60 minutes and can only be used once. Choose a new password with at least 12 characters. If your workspace uses single sign-on (SSO), you can't reset your password in Relaydesk — reset it with your identity provider instead.

Step by step

  1. Open app.relaydesk.example and click Forgot password? under the sign-in form.
  2. Enter the email address you use for Relaydesk and click Send reset link.
  3. Open the email and click Reset password within 60 minutes.
  4. Enter a new password of at least 12 characters and confirm it.
  5. Sign in with your new password. If you use two-factor authentication, you'll still be asked for your code or security key.

Resetting your password signs you out of Relaydesk on every other browser and device, including the mobile agent apps.

Tip: A long passphrase of four or five unrelated words is easier to remember than a short, complex password and easily meets the 12-character minimum.

Didn't receive the email?

  • Wait a few minutes, then check your spam, junk or promotions folder.
  • Check the address. Make sure you entered the email you were invited with. For security, we show the same confirmation message whether or not the address has an account.
  • Ask IT about quarantine. Some company mail filters hold automated emails. Ask your IT team to allow mail from relaydesk.example.
  • Request a new link. Each new request makes earlier links stop working, so always use the most recent email.
  • Check for SSO. If your workspace uses single sign-on, no reset email is sent. Reset your password with your identity provider.

If none of this works, ask a workspace Admin to send you a reset, or email support@relaydesk.example from the address you use for Relaydesk.

Admin-forced password reset

Admins can require a member to choose a new password, for example if a laptop is lost or a password may have been shared.

  1. Go to Settings → Team and open the member's profile.
  2. Click Force password reset and confirm.

The member is signed out everywhere immediately and receives a reset email. The usual rules apply: the link expires after 60 minutes and can only be used once. The action is recorded in the audit log. Admins can't see or set another member's password.

Locked account

After 10 failed sign-in attempts, Relaydesk locks the account to protect it. The account unlocks automatically after 30 minutes. You don't need to contact anyone: wait 30 minutes and try again, or reset your password using the steps above. If you didn't make those attempts yourself, reset your password and tell your workspace Admin.
