Invite teammates to your workspace

Owners and Admins can invite people one at a time, in bulk from a CSV file, or automatically through SCIM on the Scale plan.

Last updated July 8, 2026 · 3 min read

Only Owners and Admins can invite people. Each person you invite gets one role: Admin, Agent or Light seat. Admins and Agents use a paid agent seat. Light seats are free on all plans: they can view tickets and add internal notes, but can't reply to customers. For a full comparison of roles, see the workspace administration guide.

Invite one person

  1. Go to Settings → Team and click Invite.
  2. Enter the person's work email address. You can paste several addresses separated by commas.
  3. Choose a role: Admin, Agent or Light seat.
  4. Optionally add them to one or more teams, so they start receiving assignments straight away.
  5. Click Send invitation.

The person receives an email with a link to join. They set their name and a password of at least 12 characters, and, if your workspace requires it, set up two-factor authentication before they can see any tickets.

Tip: Invite people who only need to answer questions from agents, such as engineers or warehouse staff, as light seats. They can be @mentioned in internal notes and don't cost anything.

Invitation expiry and resending

Invitation links expire after 7 days. Pending invitations are listed under Settings → Team → Pending with the date they were sent.

  • Resend: click Resend next to a pending or expired invitation. The person gets a new link that is valid for another 7 days, and the old link stops working.
  • Revoke: click Revoke to cancel an invitation that was sent to the wrong address.

If someone says they never got the email, ask them to check spam and any quarantine folder their IT team uses, then resend. A pending invitation reserves a seat until it is accepted, revoked or expires.

Seat limits and billing

The Starter plan allows up to 10 agent seats and up to 50 light seats. If you try to invite an 11th Admin or Agent on Starter, Relaydesk asks you to upgrade or invite the person as a light seat instead. Growth and Scale have no agent seat limit.

On Growth and Scale, inviting an Admin or Agent when all your paid seats are in use adds a seat. Seats added mid-cycle are prorated, so you pay only for the rest of the current billing period. Removing a member frees their seat immediately, but the seat itself is removed from your bill at the next renewal. See Billing and invoices.

Bulk invite with a CSV file

To invite a whole team at once, upload a CSV file from Settings → Team → Invite → Bulk invite. Each file can contain up to 200 rows, one person per row. For more people, split the list into several files.

The file needs a header row with these columns:

ColumnRequiredExample
emailYesjordan.lee@northwind.example
nameNoJordan Lee
roleYesadmin, agent or light
teamsNoBilling; Tier 2

Before anything is sent, Relaydesk shows a preview with any problems highlighted, such as invalid email addresses, people who are already members, or rows that would take you over the Starter seat limit. Fix the file and upload it again, or skip the flagged rows and send the rest.

Automatic provisioning with SCIM

On the Scale plan, you can connect your identity provider with SCIM so people are added, updated and deactivated automatically. When someone joins the support group in your identity provider, they appear in Relaydesk with the role you've mapped to that group. When they leave, they're deactivated and their seat is freed.

  1. Set up SAML single sign-on first under Settings → Security → Single sign-on.
  2. Go to Settings → Security → SCIM and generate a SCIM token.
  3. Paste the SCIM base URL and token into your identity provider.
  4. Map identity provider groups to Relaydesk roles and teams.
Note: When SCIM is on, manage members from your identity provider. Changes made by hand in Relaydesk may be overwritten at the next sync.
