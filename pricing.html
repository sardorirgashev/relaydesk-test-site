Pricing FAQ

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period, so you keep the higher plan until then.

What happens when I add or remove seats?

Seats you add mid-cycle are prorated for the rest of the period. Seats you remove are taken off at your next renewal.

How do I pay?

All plans accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Annual Scale plans can also pay by invoice, ACH or wire transfer.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. Monthly plans can get a full refund of the first paid charge within 30 days, and annual plans within 30 days of purchase or renewal. See the Refund Policy for details.

What counts as an AI resolution?

A conversation that Relay AI Autopilot closes with no human reply in the following 72 hours. Conversations Autopilot hands to an agent are not counted.
