What’s new

Release notes

What we shipped, newest first. Relaydesk releases a named version roughly every two months; fixes ship continuously in between.

Last updated October 14, 2026 · 1 min read

2026.10 · October 14, 2026

New

  • Relay AI Autopilot on WhatsApp. Autopilot can now answer WhatsApp conversations on Growth and Scale, with the same handoff rules as chat and email.
  • SLA breach forecasts. Tickets likely to breach their SLA in the next 2 hours are flagged in views, based on queue size and agent availability.
  • Dark mode in the web and mobile agent apps.

Improved

  • Macro search now matches macro content, not only titles.
  • CSV exports include custom field labels as column headers.

2026.08 · August 19, 2026

New

  • Knowledge Base article versioning. Every published change is saved; compare versions side by side and restore any of them.
  • 18 translation languages for Knowledge Base articles, up from 12. New: Polish, Turkish, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Hebrew and Ukrainian.

Fixed

  • Chat widget pre-chat forms no longer reset when a visitor switches tabs.

2026.06 · June 10, 2026

  • SCIM provisioning for Scale workspaces with Okta, Microsoft Entra ID and other SCIM 2.0 identity providers.
  • Audit log export to CSV and JSON.
  • Updated Privacy Policy with a refreshed sub-processor list.

2026.03 · March 1, 2026

  • New refund policy. The refund window is now 30 days for both monthly and annual plans. See the Refund Policy.
  • EU data residency is generally available on Scale (AWS eu-central-1, Frankfurt).
  • Updated Terms of Service and Uptime SLA.

2025.11 · November 12, 2025

  • Relay AI Autopilot is generally available on chat and email for Growth and Scale, after a six-month beta. Growth includes 500 resolutions a month, Scale 2,000.

2024.05 · May 2024

  • Relay AI Copilot launches. Draft replies, thread summaries and tone rewrites for agents on Growth and Scale.
