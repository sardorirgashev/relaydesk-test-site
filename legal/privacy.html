Legal

Privacy Policy

What personal data Relaydesk collects, why we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how to exercise your rights.

Last updated June 10, 2026 · 6 min read

This Privacy Policy explains how Relaydesk, Inc. (“Relaydesk”, “we”) collects, uses and shares personal data when you visit relaydesk.example, sign up for a workspace or use the Relaydesk Service. It also explains the choices and rights you have.

Relaydesk plays two roles. For account, billing and website data, we are the controller. For the content our customers put into their workspaces (messages from their end users, contact records, attachments and Knowledge Base articles), we are a processor acting on our customer's instructions. If you contacted a business that uses Relaydesk, that business's own privacy notice applies, and you should send requests to them first.

Data we collect

Information you give us

  • Account data: name, work email address, company name, role and password (stored as a salted hash), plus 2FA settings.
  • Billing data: billing contact, billing address, tax ID and plan details. Card numbers are collected and stored by our payment processor, Stripe; we only see the card brand, last four digits and expiry date.
  • Support data: what you send us when you contact support@relaydesk.example or sales@relaydesk.example.

Customer Data in workspaces

Messages, emails, chats, SMS, WhatsApp and social DMs, attachments up to 25 MB per file, end-user contact details, internal notes, tags, CSAT ratings and Knowledge Base content that our customers and their end users submit.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage and device data: IP address, browser and device type, pages and features used, and timestamps.
  • Audit log events: sign-ins, setting changes and exports within a workspace.
  • Cookies: strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and secure, and, only if you allow them, analytics cookies on our marketing website. The Live Chat widget uses a first-party cookie to keep an end user's conversation together across pages.

How we use data

  • To provide the Service: deliver and route conversations, run assignment rules, SLA policies and reports, and send CSAT surveys and transcripts on your behalf.
  • To run Relay AI for your workspace: Copilot drafts and summaries, and Autopilot answers based on your Knowledge Base. Relay AI is never trained on customer data across workspaces.
  • To secure the Service: detect abuse, enforce 2FA and session policies, and investigate incidents.
  • To bill you, provide support and send service notices such as maintenance, incidents and policy changes.
  • To improve the Service using aggregated and de-identified usage statistics, for example our published benchmark reports.
  • To send product news and marketing to business contacts, which you can opt out of at any time from the link in each email.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are performance of a contract (providing the Service), legitimate interests (security, product improvement and B2B marketing), consent (non-essential cookies) and legal obligation (tax and accounting records).

We may disclose data if required by law, and in connection with a merger or acquisition, subject to this policy. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Retention

  • Customer Data is kept while the workspace is active and according to the customer's settings. When a workspace is deleted, we purge it from live systems within 30 days, and backups (taken every 6 hours and retained for 35 days) age out within a further 35 days.
  • Audit logs are kept for 30 days on Starter, 90 days on Growth and 1 year on Scale.
  • Billing records are kept for 7 years to meet tax and accounting obligations.
  • Workspace exports that admins generate are available to download for 7 days, then deleted.

Relay AI and automated processing

When a workspace turns on Relay AI, the text of the relevant conversation and the matching Knowledge Base articles are sent to our AI model provider to generate a draft, a summary or an Autopilot reply. The provider works under a zero-data-retention agreement: it does not store prompts or outputs after the response is returned, and it does not use them to train its models. Relay AI is never trained on customer data across workspaces.

Relay AI does not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about anyone. Autopilot only answers support questions from the customer's own help content, and it hands the conversation to a human when its confidence is low or when the end user asks for a person. End users who prefer to talk to a human can say so at any point in the conversation.

Your choices

  • Marketing email: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or write to privacy@relaydesk.example. Service notices, such as incident and billing emails, are still sent while you have an account.
  • Cookies: non-essential analytics cookies on our website are off until you accept them in the cookie notice, and you can change your choice at any time from the cookie settings link in the footer.
  • Workspace settings: admins control many retention and privacy settings directly, including idle session timeouts, 2FA requirements, automatic chat transcripts and data exports.

Children's data

The Service is designed for businesses and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16 for our own purposes. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact privacy@relaydesk.example and we will delete it.

Security

We encrypt data in transit with TLS 1.2+ and at rest with AES-256, hold a SOC 2 Type II report from an annual audit, and are working toward ISO 27001 certification, expected in Q2 2027. On the Scale plan we sign HIPAA Business Associate Agreements. Read more on our security page.

International transfers

Relaydesk is based in the United States, and data is hosted in AWS us-west-2 by default. Scale customers can choose EU data residency in AWS eu-central-1 (Frankfurt). When personal data from the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland is transferred to the US, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and their UK and Swiss equivalents, which are included in our Data Processing Agreement.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a supervisory authority. We respond to verified requests within 30 days.

GDPR (EEA and UK)

Our EU representative under Article 27 of the GDPR is our Lisbon office: Relaydesk, Inc., Rua do Ouro 120, 1100-063 Lisbon, Portugal. You can contact it directly or write to privacy@relaydesk.example. Customers can sign our Data Processing Agreement from the workspace settings.

CCPA (California)

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion and correction, to opt out of sale or sharing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA. You or an authorized agent can make a request by emailing privacy@relaydesk.example.

If your data is in a customer's workspace, we will pass your request to that customer and help them respond.

Contact us

Questions or requests about privacy: privacy@relaydesk.example. By mail: Relaydesk, Inc., 418 Alder Street, Suite 300, Portland, OR 97204, USA. Security issues should go to security@relaydesk.example.
