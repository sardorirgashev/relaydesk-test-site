Uptime SLA

Our availability commitment for Growth and Scale workspaces, how we measure it, and the service credits you receive if we fall short.

Last updated March 1, 2026 · 4 min read

This Uptime Service Level Agreement (“SLA”) forms part of the Relaydesk Terms of Service. It describes the availability Relaydesk, Inc. commits to for paid workspaces on the Growth and Scale plans, and the service credits you receive if we miss that commitment.

Uptime commitment

PlanMonthly uptime commitment
StarterNo SLA (best effort)
Growth99.9%
Scale99.95%

The SLA covers the Relaydesk web app and agent apps, the Shared Inbox, Live Chat (including the website widget), Knowledge Base help centers, Relay AI and the public API. Free trials are not covered.

How uptime is measured

Monthly uptime is the percentage of minutes in a calendar month (UTC) during which the Service is available, calculated as:

Monthly uptime % = (total minutes in the month − minutes of downtime) ÷ total minutes in the month × 100

A minute counts as downtime when our external monitoring shows that agents cannot sign in or load tickets, or that the API or Live Chat widget returns server errors for more than 5% of requests during that minute. Minutes excluded under “Exclusions” below are not counted as downtime.

For example, a 30-day month has 43,200 minutes. The 99.9% Growth commitment allows about 43 minutes of downtime in that month; the 99.95% Scale commitment allows about 21 minutes. We publish live status and incident history at status.relaydesk.example.

Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below your plan's commitment, you are eligible for a service credit calculated as a percentage of that month's fee for the affected workspace:

Monthly uptimeCredit (Scale, 99.95% SLA)Credit (Growth, 99.9% SLA)
Below the SLA target but ≥ 99.0%10%10%
< 99.0% but ≥ 95.0%25%25%
< 95.0%50%50%

Service credits are capped at 50% of the monthly fee for the affected month. For annual plans, the monthly fee is one twelfth of the annual subscription fee. Credits are calculated on seat fees only, not on usage-based charges such as extra AI resolutions or SMS.

Credits are applied to your next invoice or renewal. They are not refundable as cash, cannot be transferred to another workspace, and are your sole and exclusive remedy for unavailability of the Service.

Exclusions

Downtime does not include unavailability caused by:

  • Scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance on status.relaydesk.example, held on Sundays between 02:00 and 06:00 UTC, and limited to a maximum of 4 hours per month. Maintenance beyond 4 hours in a month counts as downtime.
  • Outages of third-party channels and networks outside our control, such as email providers, mobile carriers, WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram, or your DNS settings for a custom sender or help center domain.
  • Integrations provided by third parties, including Shopify, Slack, Salesforce and Zapier.
  • Your own equipment, internet connection, browser extensions or code, including modifications to the Live Chat widget snippet.
  • Suspension of your workspace under the Terms of Service, for example for non-payment or a breach of acceptable use.
  • Requests that exceed your plan's API rate limits (600 requests per minute on Growth, 1,200 on Scale).
  • Events beyond our reasonable control, such as natural disasters, acts of government or large-scale internet failures not caused by Relaydesk.
  • Features labeled beta, preview or early access.

How to claim a credit

  1. A workspace admin emails support@relaydesk.example with the subject line “SLA credit request” within 30 days of the end of the month in which the downtime occurred. Claims received later are not eligible.
  2. Include your workspace name, the dates and times (with time zone) of the downtime you experienced, and a short description of the impact, for example error messages or affected channels.
  3. We check your claim against our monitoring data and status page records and reply within 10 business days.
  4. If the claim is approved, the credit appears on your next invoice or renewal. If we decline it, we explain why and share the uptime figure we measured for that month.
Tip: Subscribe to updates on status.relaydesk.example so you have a record of incidents and maintenance windows when you prepare a claim.

During an incident

If you think Relaydesk is unavailable, check status.relaydesk.example first. If no incident is listed, contact support@relaydesk.example. Scale customers have 24/7 support with a first reply to urgent issues within 1 hour; Growth customers have chat and email support 24 hours a day, 5 days a week, with a first reply within 4 hours. We post updates on the status page throughout an incident, and for significant incidents we publish a short summary of the cause and the steps we are taking to prevent it from happening again. Reporting an incident to support does not count as an SLA claim; please still follow the claim process above.

Changes to this SLA

We may update this SLA with at least 30 days' notice to workspace admins. Changes will not reduce the uptime commitment or credit levels during an annual term that is already paid. Questions about this SLA can go to support@relaydesk.example, and plan details are on the pricing page.
