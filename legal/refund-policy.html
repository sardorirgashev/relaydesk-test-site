Refund Policy

When you can get a refund for a Relaydesk subscription, what is not refundable, and how to ask. Effective March 1, 2026.

Last updated March 1, 2026 · 3 min read

We want you to be confident when you choose Relaydesk. This Refund Policy explains when you can get your money back for a Relaydesk subscription and how to ask for it. It forms part of the Terms of Service.

Effective March 1, 2026. This policy replaces our previous refund policy and applies to all charges made on or after that date.

Start with the free trial

Every new workspace begins with a 14-day free trial that includes Growth plan features and 50 AI resolutions. No credit card is needed, so you are never charged during the trial and there is nothing to refund. We encourage you to use the trial to test your channels, Live Chat and Relay AI before you buy.

Monthly plans

If you pay monthly, you can get a full refund of your first paid charge if you request it within 30 days of that charge. This applies once per workspace, on any plan (Starter, Growth or Scale).

Later monthly charges are not refunded. If you cancel after your first month, your subscription simply ends at the close of the current billing period, and you keep access until then.

Annual plans

If you pay annually, you can get a full refund within 30 days of purchase or renewal. The same 30-day window applies to your first annual payment and to each annual renewal charge.

After 30 days, annual charges are not refunded, but you keep full access to your plan until the end of your term. You can turn off auto-renewal at any time from Billing so the plan does not renew.

What is never refunded

  • Extra AI resolutions: usage beyond the 500 (Growth) or 2,000 (Scale) AI resolutions included each month, billed at $0.60 each.
  • SMS usage charges for messages sent and received.

Usage charges are excluded because they pay for messages and AI answers that have already been delivered to your customers.

Removing seats or downgrading your plan does not create a refund or credit. Those changes take effect at the end of your current billing period. Service credits for downtime are handled separately under the Uptime SLA and are applied to future invoices rather than paid as refunds.

How to request a refund

  1. A workspace admin opens Billing in the workspace settings and clicks Request refund next to the charge. You can also email billing@relaydesk.example from the billing contact address with your workspace name and the invoice number.
  2. We confirm eligibility and reply by email, usually within 2 business days.
  3. Approved refunds go back to the original card. They reach your card in 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

When we issue a full refund, the related subscription is cancelled. Export any data you want to keep before you request a refund; see Export your data.

Annual Scale customers who paid by invoice, ACH or wire receive refunds by bank transfer to the account that paid. Please contact billing@relaydesk.example before disputing a charge with your bank, so we can resolve it quickly.

Examples

  • You start a Growth plan on monthly billing on April 3 after your trial. On April 20 you decide it isn't right for your team. Your request is within 30 days of your first paid charge, so you get a full refund of that charge.
  • You are on monthly billing and ask for a refund of your third monthly charge. Only the first paid charge is refundable, so this charge is not refunded, but you can cancel so you aren't charged again.
  • Your annual Scale plan renews on June 1. You ask for a refund on June 25. That is within 30 days of renewal, so the renewal charge is refunded in full.

Questions

For anything not covered here, email billing@relaydesk.example or read Billing and invoices. Details of each plan are on the pricing page.
