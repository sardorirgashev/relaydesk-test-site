Integrations · Slack

Support conversations, right where your team talks.

The Relaydesk Slack integration posts new tickets to the channels you choose, lets you reply from Slack threads, and turns any request into a ticket with /relay.

Start free trialAll integrations

What it does

Three ways Slack and Relaydesk work together.

New-ticket notifications

Post a message to a Slack channel whenever a new ticket arrives, so the right people see urgent requests quickly.

Reply from threads

Reply to a ticket from the notification's Slack thread. The reply is added to the ticket in Relaydesk.

The /relay command

Type /relay in Slack to create a ticket, for example when a colleague passes on a customer request in chat.

Plan availability

Available on every plan.

The Slack integration is included on Starter, Growth and Scale at no extra cost. Compare plans.

Who it is for

The Slack integration helps teams where not everyone works in the helpdesk all day. Operations, engineering or account managers can watch a channel for new tickets, jump into a thread when they know the answer, and log a customer request with /relay the moment they hear about it.

Pairs well with light seats

Light (collaborator) seats are free on all plans and can view tickets and add internal notes, so colleagues who follow tickets in Slack can also add context inside Relaydesk. Starter allows up to 50 light seats.

Setup

Set up in a few minutes.

  1. Open integrations

    In Relaydesk, go to Settings, then Integrations, and choose Slack.

  2. Authorize Slack

    Sign in to your Slack workspace and approve the app's permissions.

  3. Pick channels

    Choose which Slack channels receive new-ticket notifications.

  4. Try it out

    Reply in a notification thread, or type /relay to create a ticket.

What syncs

What moves between Slack and Relaydesk.

Limitations

  • Notifications are sent for new tickets. For other events, use one of your workspace's 20 webhooks.
  • Only full agent seats can reply to customers; light (collaborator) seats can view tickets and add internal notes but cannot reply.
  • The 25 MB per-file attachment limit applies to all tickets.

FAQ

Slack integration questions

Is the Slack integration included on Starter?

Yes. Slack is available on all plans: Starter, Growth and Scale.

Can I reply to customers from Slack?

Yes. Reply in the Slack thread of a new-ticket notification and the reply is added to the ticket in Relaydesk.

How do I turn a Slack message into a ticket?

Use the /relay slash command to create a new Relaydesk ticket from Slack.

Can I get Slack alerts for events other than new tickets?

The Slack integration notifies on new tickets. For other events, use webhooks (up to 20 per workspace) or Zapier, which is available on all plans.
