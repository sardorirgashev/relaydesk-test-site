Webhooks and API

Build exactly the integration you need.

Webhooks send Relaydesk events to your own endpoints in real time. Each workspace can have up to 20 webhooks. If your endpoint is down or returns an error, Relaydesk retries the delivery 5 times with exponential backoff over about 1 hour.

The REST API gives you access to tickets, contacts and more. Starter workspaces get read-only access; Growth and Scale can read and write. Admins can also generate a full workspace export in CSV or JSON, available for 7 days after generation.
