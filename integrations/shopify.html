Integrations · Shopify

Handle Shopify orders without leaving the ticket.

The Relaydesk Shopify integration puts a customer's recent orders next to their message and lets agents refund, cancel or fix an address in a couple of clicks.

Start free trialAll integrations

What it does

Order context and order actions, in one place.

Order sidebar

See the customer's last 5 orders beside the conversation, so nobody has to ask for an order number twice.

Refund, cancel, edit address

Refund an order, cancel it or edit the shipping address directly from the ticket.

Order-status macros

Insert order-status variables into macros so “where is my order?” replies fill themselves in.

Plan availability

Available on Growth and Scale.

The Shopify integration is included on the Growth and Scale plans and is not available on Starter. The 14-day free trial includes Growth features, so you can try the integration before you buy. Compare plans.

Who it is for

The Shopify integration is built for online stores whose support queue is full of order questions: where is my package, can I change my address, can I cancel before it ships. Instead of copying an order number into the Shopify admin, agents see the order, act on it and reply from the same screen in Relaydesk.

Works with every channel

Because the order sidebar sits on the ticket, it works the same whether the customer wrote by email, Live Chat, SMS, WhatsApp or Instagram. Growth costs $49 per agent seat per month, or $39 per month billed annually.

Setup

Connected in four steps.

  1. Find the app

    Search for Relaydesk in the Shopify App Store from your Shopify admin.

  2. Install and approve

    Install the app and approve the order and customer permissions it requests.

  3. Link your workspace

    Sign in to your Relaydesk workspace when prompted to connect the store.

  4. Update your macros

    Add order-status variables to your saved replies. See macros.

Limitations

  • The sidebar shows the last 5 orders only; older orders stay in Shopify.
  • Order actions are limited to refund, cancel and edit address.
  • Not available on the Starter plan.

FAQ

Shopify integration questions

Which plans include the Shopify integration?

Growth and Scale. It is not available on Starter, but you can try it during the 14-day free trial, which includes Growth features.

Where do I install it?

From the Shopify App Store. After installing, you connect the app to your Relaydesk workspace.

How many orders can agents see?

The order sidebar shows the customer's last 5 orders.

Can agents refund or cancel orders from Relaydesk?

Yes. Agents can refund, cancel or edit the address of an order directly from the ticket.
