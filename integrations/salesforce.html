Integrations · Salesforce

Support and sales see the same customer.

The Relaydesk Salesforce integration keeps contacts and accounts in sync both ways, puts ticket history on the Salesforce record, and turns Salesforce cases into Relaydesk tickets.

Talk to salesAll integrations

What it does

One customer record, two teams.

Two-way sync

Contacts and accounts stay in sync between Relaydesk and Salesforce in both directions, every 5 minutes.

Ticket history on the record

Account owners see a customer's Relaydesk ticket history on the Salesforce record before a renewal call.

Tickets from cases

Create a Relaydesk ticket from a Salesforce case, so support picks it up in the Shared Inbox.

Plan availability

Available on Scale only.

The Salesforce integration is included on the Scale plan and is not available on Starter or Growth. Scale customers with 25 or more seats also get a dedicated customer success manager who can help plan the rollout. Compare plans.

Who it is for

The Salesforce integration is for companies where sales and account teams live in Salesforce and support lives in Relaydesk. Two-way sync means a contact updated in either system is updated in the other, and account owners see support history without asking for a report.

Part of the Scale plan

Scale costs $89 per agent seat per month, or $71 per month billed annually. It also includes SSO with SAML 2.0 and SCIM, EU data residency, a HIPAA BAA, 2,000 Autopilot resolutions a month and API access at 1,200 requests per minute.

Setup

From connection to first sync.

  1. Open integrations

    In your Scale workspace, go to Settings, then Integrations, and choose Salesforce.

  2. Authorize Salesforce

    Sign in with a Salesforce administrator account and approve access.

  3. Review the sync

    Confirm that contacts and accounts should sync both ways, and choose whether to create tickets from cases.

  4. Wait for the first sync

    The integration syncs every 5 minutes. Ticket history then appears on Salesforce records.

What syncs

Objects, direction and timing.

ItemDirectionTiming
ContactsTwo-wayEvery 5 minutes
AccountsTwo-wayEvery 5 minutes
Ticket historyRelaydesk to Salesforce recordEvery 5 minutes
CasesSalesforce case to Relaydesk ticketEvery 5 minutes

Limitations

  • Sync runs every 5 minutes, not instantly, so a change can take up to about 5 minutes to appear.
  • Two-way sync covers contacts and accounts; other Salesforce objects are not synced.
  • Available on the Scale plan only.

FAQ

Salesforce integration questions

Which plan includes the Salesforce integration?

Only Scale. It is not available on Starter or Growth.

How often does data sync?

Every 5 minutes. A change made in either system can take up to about 5 minutes to appear in the other.

What data syncs?

Contacts and accounts sync both ways. Relaydesk ticket history appears on the Salesforce record, and Salesforce cases can create Relaydesk tickets.

Can I connect Salesforce on Growth through the API instead?

The native integration is Scale only. On Growth you can build your own connection using the API (600 requests per minute) and up to 20 webhooks.
