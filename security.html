Trust & security

Security at Relaydesk

Your customers trust you with their messages. Here is how we protect them: the certifications we hold, where data lives, and the controls you have.

Last updated June 10, 2026 · 3 min read

Certifications and compliance

Relaydesk is independently audited every year against the SOC 2 Type II trust services criteria for security, availability and confidentiality. Customers and prospects under NDA can request the latest report from security@relaydesk.example.

  • SOC 2 Type II, audited annually.
  • GDPR and CCPA: we act as a data processor and sign a Data Processing Agreement with every customer on request.
  • HIPAA: a Business Associate Agreement is available on the Scale plan for healthcare customers.
  • ISO/IEC 27001: certification is in progress and expected in Q2 2027.

Where your data lives

Relaydesk runs on Amazon Web Services. By default, workspaces are hosted in AWS us-west-2 (Oregon). Customers on the Scale plan can choose EU data residency, which keeps ticket content, attachments, Knowledge Base articles and backups in AWS eu-central-1 (Frankfurt). EU residency became generally available in March 2026.

Moving regions: an existing Scale workspace can be migrated to the EU region. Contact your customer success manager to schedule it; migrations run during the Sunday maintenance window.

Encryption

All traffic to Relaydesk, including the chat widget and the API, uses TLS 1.2 or higher. Data at rest, including attachments and backups, is encrypted with AES-256. Encryption keys are managed in AWS KMS and rotated annually.

Backups and recovery

We take encrypted backups every 6 hours and keep them for 35 days. Backups are stored in a separate AWS account from production. We test restores every quarter.

When you delete a workspace, its data is purged from production within 30 days, and backups containing it age out within 35 days after that.

Our own access to your data

Relaydesk employees can access a workspace only to resolve a support request, and only after the workspace owner grants temporary access from Settings → Security → Support access. Access expires automatically after 72 hours, and every action is recorded in your audit log. Production access for engineers requires hardware-key 2FA and is reviewed quarterly.

Relay AI and your data

Relay AI generates answers using your workspace’s Knowledge Base and conversation history. Your data is never used to train models that serve other workspaces. Our AI model provider processes requests under a zero-data-retention agreement.

Availability

Growth customers are covered by a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA and Scale customers by 99.95%, with service credits if we miss them. Scheduled maintenance is announced at least 72 hours ahead, is limited to 4 hours a month and happens on Sundays between 02:00 and 06:00 UTC. Read the full Uptime SLA.

Report a vulnerability

If you believe you’ve found a security issue, email security@relaydesk.example with steps to reproduce. We acknowledge reports within 2 business days and reward valid findings through our bug bounty program. Please don’t access other customers’ data or degrade the service while testing.
