Relay AI

AI that knows when to step back.

Relay AI Autopilot answers on chat, email and now WhatsApp using only your Knowledge Base. When its confidence is low, when the topic is sensitive, or when a customer simply asks for a person, it hands the conversation to your team with a summary attached.

How Relay AI works

Grounded in your articles

Answers cite the Knowledge Base articles they came from, so agents can check them in one click.

Your data stays yours

Relay AI is never trained on one workspace’s conversations to answer another’s.

Priced per resolution

Growth includes 500 AI resolutions a month and Scale includes 2,000. Extra resolutions are $0.60 each.

How teams get started

Live in an afternoon.

  1. Forward your support email

    Point support@yourcompany to your Relaydesk forwarding address and tickets start arriving in minutes.

  2. Paste the chat snippet

    One script tag adds Live Chat to your site. It loads asynchronously and never blocks your page.

  3. Import your FAQs

    Turn existing help pages into Knowledge Base articles that customers and Relay AI can both use.

  4. Invite your team

    Add agents and free light seats, set business hours, and switch on an SLA policy.
