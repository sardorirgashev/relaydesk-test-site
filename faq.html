General

What is Relaydesk?

Relaydesk is an omnichannel helpdesk for small and mid-size businesses. It brings email, SMS, WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram DMs, and live chat into one shared queue so your team can answer every customer from a single, calm inbox.

Relaydesk, Inc. was founded in 2019 in Portland, Oregon, by Maya Okafor (CEO) and Daniel Reyes (CTO). Today more than 6,000 businesses in over 40 countries run their support on Relaydesk. See the product overview for a tour.

Which products are included in Relaydesk?

Relaydesk is made up of four products that share one workspace:

  • Shared Inbox for email, SMS, WhatsApp and social DMs in one queue.
  • Live Chat, an embeddable website widget with mobile SDKs.
  • Knowledge Base, a public help center on your own domain.
  • Relay AI, the AI assistant with Copilot and Autopilot modes.

Reporting and SLAs (dashboards, SLA policies, CSAT) are built into all four rather than sold separately.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new workspace starts with a 14-day free trial that includes all Growth plan features, so you can test SMS, WhatsApp, social channels and Relay AI Copilot alongside email and live chat.

No credit card is required to start. The trial also includes 50 Relay AI Autopilot resolutions, which is enough to see how Autopilot handles real customer questions from your Knowledge Base. Compare what each plan includes on the pricing page.

Does Relaydesk have mobile apps?

Yes. Agents can work tickets on the go with the Relaydesk agent apps for iOS and Android, which support the same shared queue, internal notes and assignment as the web app. The agent app also offers dark mode.

Separately, if you want to offer chat inside your own mobile app, Live Chat ships mobile SDKs for iOS and Android. Conversations from the SDKs land in the same inbox as your website chats. Learn more on the Live Chat page.

Where is Relaydesk based?

Relaydesk is headquartered at 418 Alder Street, Suite 300, Portland, OR 97204, USA. Our EU office is at Rua do Ouro 120, 1100-063 Lisbon, Portugal.

The company has about 140 employees across both offices. Customer data is hosted on AWS in us-west-2 (Oregon) by default, and Scale plan workspaces can choose EU data residency in AWS eu-central-1 (Frankfurt). Read more about Relaydesk or find all our contact details on the contact page.

How do I get started with Relaydesk?

Most teams set up their workspace in three steps: connect a support email address, install the Live Chat widget on their website, and invite teammates. The getting started guide walks through each step.

From there you can add assignment rules, macros and SLA policies. If you are the workspace admin and want every setting in one place, the complete admin guide covers channels, business hours, roles, security and Relay AI from start to finish.

Pricing & billing

How much does Relaydesk cost?

Relaydesk is priced per agent seat, in US dollars, on three plans:

  • Starter: $19 per seat per month, or $15 billed annually. Up to 10 agent seats.
  • Growth: $49 per seat per month, or $39 billed annually. Unlimited seats.
  • Scale: $89 per seat per month, or $71 billed annually. Unlimited seats.

Prices exclude tax. Growth adds SMS, WhatsApp, social channels and Relay AI; Scale adds SSO, EU data residency and a HIPAA BAA. See the full comparison on the pricing page.

How much do I save with annual billing?

Annual billing saves about 20% (rounded) compared with paying month to month. On annual billing the per-seat price works out to $15 per month on Starter (instead of $19), $39 on Growth (instead of $49) and $71 on Scale (instead of $89).

Annual plans are charged up front for the full year. Annual Scale customers can also pay by invoice, ACH or wire transfer instead of card.

What are light seats, and do they cost extra?

Light seats (also called collaborator seats) are free on every plan. A light seat can view tickets and add internal notes, for example to answer a question from an agent, but cannot reply to customers.

They work well for people in engineering, finance or operations who need to see customer conversations occasionally. Growth and Scale have no light-seat limit; Starter allows up to 50 light seats. To invite someone as a light seat, follow Invite teammates.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All plans can be paid by credit or debit card: Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Invoice, ACH and wire transfer are available only on annual Scale plans.

Prices on the pricing page exclude tax, and applicable sales tax or VAT is added to your invoice. You can download past invoices and update your card at any time; see Billing and invoices for the steps.

What happens when I add or remove seats, or change plans?

Seats you add in the middle of a billing cycle are prorated, so you only pay for the remaining part of the period. Seats you remove stay active and billed until your next renewal, when the lower seat count takes effect.

Upgrading to a higher plan applies straight away. Downgrading to a lower plan takes effect at the end of your current billing period, and you keep the features of your current plan until then. More detail is in Billing and invoices.

What is your refund policy?

Under our current refund policy, effective March 1, 2026:

  • Monthly plans: we give a full refund of your first paid charge if you request it within 30 days. Later monthly charges are not refunded.
  • Annual plans: we give a full refund within 30 days of purchase or renewal. After 30 days there is no refund, but you keep access until the end of your term.

Extra AI resolutions and SMS usage are never refunded. Request a refund from Billing → “Request refund” or email billing@relaydesk.example. Refunds reach your card in 5–10 business days. Read the full Refund Policy.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or schools?

Yes. Registered nonprofits and education institutions get 40% off the Growth and Scale plans on annual billing. For example, Growth at $39 per seat per month on annual billing becomes $23.40 per seat per month with the discount.

The discount does not apply to monthly billing or to the Starter plan. To apply, email sales@relaydesk.example from your organization's domain with proof of nonprofit or education status, or use the contact page.

Channels & features

Which channels does Relaydesk support?

Relaydesk handles email, live chat, SMS, WhatsApp, and Facebook and Instagram DMs, all in one Shared Inbox queue.

Email and Live Chat are available on every plan. SMS, WhatsApp and social channels require the Growth or Scale plan. Starter includes 2 email inboxes, Growth includes 10, and Scale has unlimited inboxes. Attachments up to 25 MB per file are supported on both email and chat.

How do I connect my support email address?

Set up forwarding from your existing support address (for example help@yourcompany.com) to your workspace's inbound address, support-<workspace>@inbound.relaydesk.example. New emails then arrive in Relaydesk as tickets.

To send replies from your own domain, add the SPF and DKIM records Relaydesk shows you to your DNS. Adding a DMARC record is recommended. Full instructions are in Connect your support email.

Will the Live Chat widget slow down my website?

The Live Chat widget is about 38 KB gzipped and loads asynchronously, so it does not block the rest of your page from rendering.

The widget is available in 32 languages and supports proactive messages and pre-chat forms. When no agents are online, offline mode turns new chats into email tickets so nothing is lost. Chat transcripts can be emailed to the customer automatically. See Install the chat widget for the snippet and setup steps.

How do I stop two agents from replying to the same ticket?

Relaydesk has built-in collision detection. When a teammate opens a ticket and starts typing, everyone else viewing it sees a live indicator such as “Ana is replying…”, so two people don't send different answers to the same customer.

To coordinate without the customer seeing, leave an internal note and @mention a colleague; they get a notification and can reply in the note thread. Assignment rules also help by giving each ticket a clear owner. Learn more about the Shared Inbox.

How do assignment rules work?

Assignment rules route new tickets to agents automatically. Relaydesk supports three methods:

  • Round-robin: tickets go to each available agent in turn.
  • Load-balanced: tickets go to the available agent with the fewest open tickets.
  • Skills-based: tickets go to agents with a matching skill, such as a language or product area.

You can combine rules with tags and views to build separate queues per team. See Set up assignment rules.

When do solved tickets close?

By default, a solved ticket closes automatically after 4 days without a reply from the customer. Admins can change this to anywhere from 1 to 28 days.

If the customer replies while a ticket is solved, the ticket reopens and returns to the assigned agent. If they reply after the ticket has closed, Relaydesk creates a new ticket that is linked to the original, so the full history stays one click away. The CSAT survey is sent 1 hour after a ticket is solved by default.

Can I set response-time targets with SLA policies?

Yes. SLA policies let you set targets for first reply time, next reply time and full resolution time. If you attach a business hours schedule, the targets are measured in business hours only.

Starter includes 1 SLA policy; Growth and Scale include unlimited policies. Business hours schedules are limited to 1 on Starter and 5 on Growth, and are unlimited on Scale, each with its own holiday calendar. Follow Create SLA policies to set yours up.

Which tools does Relaydesk integrate with?

Relaydesk has native integrations with:

  • Shopify (Growth and above): see the last 5 orders and refund, cancel or edit addresses from the ticket.
  • Slack (all plans): ticket notifications, replies from Slack threads and the /relay command.
  • Salesforce (Scale only): two-way contact and account sync every 5 minutes.

There are also integrations for Zapier (all plans), HubSpot, Stripe, Jira, Microsoft Teams and Google Workspace sign-in. Browse them all on the integrations page.

Relay AI

What is Relay AI?

Relay AI is the AI assistant built into Relaydesk. It works in two modes:

  • Copilot helps your agents: it drafts replies for them to review and summarizes long threads.
  • Autopilot answers customers directly on chat and email using your Knowledge Base articles, and hands the conversation to a human when it isn't confident or when the customer asks.

Read more on the Relay AI page.

Which plans include Relay AI, and what does it cost?

Relay AI Copilot is included on Growth and Scale. It is not available on Starter.

Autopilot is billed per AI resolution. Growth includes 500 AI resolutions per month and Scale includes 2,000. Beyond that, each extra AI resolution costs $0.60 on both plans. Extra AI resolutions are never refunded. The 14-day free trial includes 50 AI resolutions so you can try Autopilot first. Compare plans on the pricing page.

What counts as an AI resolution?

An AI resolution is a conversation that Relay AI Autopilot closes with no human reply in the following 72 hours.

If an agent replies to the conversation within those 72 hours, for example because the customer came back with a follow-up that Autopilot handed off, it does not count as an AI resolution and doesn't use up your monthly allowance. Conversations that Autopilot hands to a human straight away are not counted either. You can see your resolution count for the month in Relay AI reporting.

What happens when Autopilot can't answer a question?

Autopilot hands the conversation off to a human agent in two situations: when its confidence in an answer is low, and when the customer asks to talk to a person.

The conversation moves into your normal queue and follows your assignment rules, so the right agent picks it up with the full history in front of them. Copilot can summarize the thread so the agent doesn't have to reread it. The best way to reduce handoffs is to fill gaps in your Knowledge Base; the “searches with no results” report shows where to start.

Is Relay AI trained on my customer conversations?

No. Relay AI is never trained on customer data across workspaces, so your tickets and articles are never used to improve answers for another business.

Autopilot answers from your own Knowledge Base articles. Requests are processed by an AI model provider under a zero-data-retention agreement, which means the provider does not keep your prompts or responses. The provider is listed with our other sub-processors in the Privacy Policy, and more security detail is on the security page.

Which channels can Autopilot answer on?

Autopilot answers customers on live chat and email. Since the 2026.10 release on October 14, 2026, Autopilot can also answer on WhatsApp.

Copilot works on every channel in the Shared Inbox, including SMS and Facebook and Instagram DMs, because it drafts replies for an agent to review rather than replying on its own. You choose which channels Autopilot is switched on for, so you can start with chat and expand once you trust the answers. See Set up Relay AI.

How do I set up Relay AI Autopilot?

Autopilot answers from your Knowledge Base, so start by making sure your most common questions have published, up-to-date articles. Internal-only articles are not shown to customers.

Then turn on Autopilot for the channels you want, review the handoff behavior, and test it with a few sample questions before going live. Keep an eye on the “searches with no results” report and article thumbs-down feedback to see which articles need work. The step-by-step guide is in Set up Relay AI.

Security & privacy

Which security and compliance standards does Relaydesk meet?

Relaydesk holds a SOC 2 Type II report, renewed through an annual audit, and supports GDPR and CCPA compliance. On the Scale plan we sign a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA), so healthcare teams can handle protected health information.

ISO 27001 certification is in progress and expected in Q2 2027. You can request our SOC 2 report and read about our controls on the security page.

Where is my data hosted?

By default, Relaydesk hosts all workspace data on Amazon Web Services in the us-west-2 region (Oregon, USA).

Scale plan customers can choose EU data residency, which stores workspace data in AWS eu-central-1 (Frankfurt, Germany). EU data residency became generally available in the 2026.03 release on March 1, 2026. It is not available on Starter or Growth. See the security page and the sub-processor list in our Privacy Policy.

How is my data encrypted and backed up?

All data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest with AES-256.

We back up workspace data every 6 hours and keep each backup for 35 days. Backups are encrypted in the same way as live data. Admins can also generate their own full workspace export in CSV or JSON at any time; see Export your data. More detail is on the security page.

Do you support single sign-on (SSO) and SCIM?

Yes, on the Scale plan. Relaydesk supports single sign-on with any SAML 2.0 identity provider, and SCIM provisioning to create, update and deactivate agents automatically from your identity provider. SCIM was added in the 2026.06 release.

When SSO is on, people sign in through your identity provider and their passwords are managed there, not in Relaydesk. Google Workspace sign-in is also available as an integration. SSO and SCIM are not included on Starter or Growth.

What two-factor authentication options are available?

Relaydesk supports two kinds of two-factor authentication (2FA): time-based one-time codes from an authenticator app (TOTP), and hardware security keys using WebAuthn.

Each user can turn 2FA on from their profile, and admins can require 2FA for everyone in the workspace. Passwords must be at least 12 characters. Setup steps are in Set up two-factor authentication.

What happens to my data if I delete my workspace?

When a workspace is deleted, we purge its data from our live systems within 30 days. Copies in backups age out within a further 35 days, after which no copy remains.

Deletion can't be undone, so export anything you need first. Admins can generate a full workspace export in CSV or JSON, and the export file stays available to download for 7 days after it is generated. See Export your data and our Privacy Policy.

How do I report a security vulnerability?

Please email security@relaydesk.example with a description of the issue, the steps to reproduce it and any proof-of-concept details. Valid reports are eligible for our bug bounty program.

Please do not access other customers' data, run denial-of-service tests or publicly disclose the issue before we have had a chance to fix it. For questions about our security program in general, see the security page.

Account & support

How do I reset my password?

To reset your password, go to the sign-in page at app.relaydesk.example and click “Forgot password?”. Enter the email address you use for Relaydesk and we'll email you a reset link. The link expires after 60 minutes and can only be used once. Choose a new password with at least 12 characters. If your workspace uses single sign-on (SSO), you can't reset your password in Relaydesk — reset it with your identity provider instead.

How do I invite teammates to my workspace?

Workspace admins can invite teammates by email and choose whether each person gets a full agent seat or a free light seat. Agents can reply to customers; light seats can view tickets and add internal notes only.

Starter allows up to 10 agent seats and 50 light seats; Growth and Scale have no seat limit. Agent seats you add mid-cycle are prorated. On Scale, you can also provision users automatically with SCIM. Step-by-step instructions are in Invite teammates.

How do I contact Relaydesk support, and how fast will you reply?

Email support@relaydesk.example. Response times depend on your plan:

  • Starter: email support, first reply within 24 business hours.
  • Growth: chat and email, 24 hours a day, 5 days a week, first reply within 4 hours.
  • Scale: 24/7 support with a first reply to urgent issues within 1 hour. Scale customers with 25 or more seats also get a dedicated customer success manager.

For sales questions, call +1 (503) 555-0142 (Mon–Fri, 8am–6pm Pacific) or see the contact page.

How do I export my data?

Workspace admins can generate a full export of the workspace in CSV or JSON format. The export includes tickets, conversations, contacts and Knowledge Base content.

Once the export is ready, the download is available for 7 days after it is generated; after that you need to generate a new one. Admins can also export the audit log, which is retained for 30 days on Starter, 90 days on Growth and 1 year on Scale. Follow Export your data for the steps.

Why was I signed out of Relaydesk?

For security, Relaydesk signs you out after 14 days without any activity. Workspace admins can change the idle session timeout to anywhere from 1 to 30 days, so your workspace may use a shorter setting.

If your workspace uses single sign-on, your identity provider may also end your session according to its own rules. If you are signed out more often than expected, ask your workspace admin which timeout is configured. To sign back in without your password, use “Forgot password?” or see Reset your password.

Where can I find my invoices?

Workspace admins can view and download every invoice from the Billing area of the workspace settings. The same area lets you update your payment card, change your billing email, switch between monthly and annual billing and request a refund.

Annual Scale customers who pay by invoice, ACH or wire also receive invoices by email. If you need a copy of an older invoice or a change to your billing details, contact billing@relaydesk.example. See Billing and invoices.

How can I tell whether Relaydesk is down?

Check our status page at status.relaydesk.example. It shows the current state of each part of the service, ongoing incidents and scheduled maintenance, and you can subscribe to updates.

Scheduled maintenance is announced at least 72 hours in advance and happens on Sundays between 02:00 and 06:00 UTC, for no more than 4 hours a month. Growth (99.9%) and Scale (99.95%) plans are covered by an uptime SLA with service credits; see the Uptime SLA.
